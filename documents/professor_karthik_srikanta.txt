BIO / RESEARCH SUMMARY:
Theory specialist with an NSF CAREER award. Research focuses on computational complexity, combinatorics, and algorithms. Known in the department as a rigorous theory instructor.

TEACHING STYLE PATTERNS (aggregated from reviews):
- Proof-heavy courses; expects rigorous written solutions on exams and homework
- Disorganized lecture delivery, but TA office hours are more useful
- Known to allow open-note exams in some courses
- Partial credit policy: some reviews mention 50% credit for attempted problems
- Curve policy: at least 30% of class gets an A (reported for Discrete I)
- Passive-aggressive communication style noted by multiple students

NOTES:
Reviews split sharply by course level. Lower-level courses (Discrete I) are manageable and fair. Upper-level theory courses (CS452) draw consistent complaints about organization, lack of resources, and difficulty gap. Graduate students in CS513 should expect the same proof-heavy rigor as CS452 reviewers describe.
