SECTION 1 — OFFICIAL COURSE DESCRIPTION (from cs.rutgers.edu)
================================================================

Core material for Computer Science degree candidates. Discussion of representative algorithms
and data structures encountered in applications.

TOPICS:
Worst case, average case, and amortized analysis. Data structures: search trees, hash tables,
heaps, Fibonacci heaps, union-find. Algorithms: string matching, sorting and ordering
statistics, graph algorithms. NP-completeness.

================================================================
SECTION 6 — TIPS & SURVIVAL NOTES
================================================================

- Professor matters enormously for this course — check the schedule before registering
- Srikanta: proof-heavy, disorganized lectures but lenient partial credit; use TA office hours heavily
- Szegedy: brilliant but low engagement; self-study from CLRS or MIT OCW 6.006/6.046; practice exams match real exams
- Course is proof-based — brushing up on mathematical induction, big-O notation, and graph theory before Day 1 is strongly recommended
- 6–7 HW sets + midterm + final = no projects, but HW demands rigorous written proofs
- Gateway to CS514 (Algorithms II), CS538 (Complexity), CS550 (Data Mining) — foundational
