BIO / RESEARCH SUMMARY:
Professor and director of the Art and AI Lab at Rutgers. Published over 180 peer-reviewed papers in computer vision, machine learning, and AI. Known for Emmy-winning AI art research and NSF CAREER award. Strong research profile; teaching reviews are mixed with complaints about workload volume and surprise assessments.

TEACHING STYLE PATTERNS (aggregated from reviews):
- Genuinely caring and wants students to succeed — positive sentiment about his personality is consistent
- Difficult to understand due to accent (noted in multiple reviews)
- Surprise quizzes with short notice (Saturday announcement for Monday quiz)
- Lectures are 3 hours long — mentally demanding
- Very flexible on homework deadlines if you communicate proactively
- Math-heavy content: linear algebra, probability, calculus all required in practice
- Lenient grading overall despite hard assignments
- Heavy assignment load — students warn it can consume the entire semester

NOTES:
28% would take again reflects the workload shock more than the professor's intent. Students who go in knowing the course is math-heavy and assignment-dense tend to rate him better. The flexibility on deadlines is a genuine safety valve — use it. For CS536 (Machine Learning), expect the same pattern: brilliant researcher, demanding course, approachable if you engage early.
